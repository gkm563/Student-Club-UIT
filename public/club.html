About CodeCrunch

We aim to create a strong coding culture in the campus by organizing workshops, hackathons, coding contests and development projects. Our mission is to empower students to turn ideas into impact through code.

Mission

To empower students with technical skills and creativity to build innovative solutions.

Vision

To be the most active and impactful coding community in the region.

Objectives
  • • Enhance coding skills
  • • Promote teamwork
  • • Build real-world projects
  • • Encourage open source
