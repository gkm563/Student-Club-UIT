Institutional Overview

What is USC UIT?

United Student Club (USC UIT) serves as the central administrative and governance framework for co-curricular excellence at United Institute of Technology. Founded with the mandate to nurture holistic student growth, USC UIT oversees club registrations, event proposals, budget allocations, lab resources, and institutional mentorship.

Whether a student aims to solve national challenges in the Google Solution Challenge, compete in competitive coding contests, stage theatrical Nukkad Natak plays, or publish thought-provoking articles, USC UIT ensures structured support and equal opportunity across every B.Tech department.
