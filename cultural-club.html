01 / PERFORM

Music & Campus Band

Jam, rehearse, perform vocals, guitar, keyboards, and connect through campus music nights.

02 / DANCE

Nritya Dance Chapter

Bring energy to group choreography, classical, hip-hop, and big stage festival performances.

Public Speaking & Debates

Build vocal confidence through Toastmasters speech drills, debates, and stage hosting.

Explore characters, Nukkad Natak street plays, scriptwriting, and stage direction.

05 / FINE ARTS

Paint, illustrate, design, exhibit art, and capture photography for campus events.

06 / IDEAS

TEDx & Literary Society

Curate talks, write essays, host poetry slams, and share ideas worth spreading across campus.
