What we do

Choose the way you want to express yourself.

There is room for the loud, the quiet, the curious, the confident, and everyone still figuring out where they belong.

01 / PERFORM

Music & band

Jam, rehearse, perform, and connect through vocals, instruments, and campus music experiences.

02 / MOVE

Dance & stage

Bring energy to group performances, dance showcases, and the big stage moments of campus fests.

03 / SPEAK

Public speaking

Build your voice through speech sessions, debates, storytelling, hosting, and thoughtful feedback.

04 / ACT

Theatre & drama

Explore characters, scripts, direction, and the joy of bringing a story alive with a team.

05 / CREATE

Fine arts & design

Paint, illustrate, design, exhibit, and help shape the visual identity of cultural experiences.

06 / SHARE

Literary & ideas

Write, discuss, debate, host, and share stories that spark fresh conversations across campus.
