What we do

Choose the kind of tech experience you want.

Every activity has a simple purpose: help you learn a little more, meet a few more people, and make something you are proud to share.

01 / BUILD

Hackathons

Work with a team to solve a real problem, turn an idea into a prototype, and present your solution.

02 / PRACTICE

Coding & DSA

Build logic, strengthen fundamentals, and enjoy problem solving through coding rounds and practice tracks.

03 / EXPLORE

AI & emerging tech

Understand modern tools through hands-on sessions in AI, machine learning, APIs, data, and automation.

04 / CREATE

Web & app development

Design interfaces, build apps, connect backends, and learn how a real product comes together.

05 / COLLABORATE

Open source

Learn Git, contribute with others, document your work, and build a public portfolio one project at a time.

06 / CONNECT

Cloud & careers

Join study jams, expert sessions, and career conversations that connect campus learning to industry.
