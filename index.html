WHO WE ARE

What is USC UIT?

USC UIT (United Student Club — UIT) is the central governing body for all student activities at United Institute of Technology. Every club, chapter, hackathon, cultural fest, and leadership program on campus operates under USC UIT.

Executive Committee

Vision of the Executive Committee

Under the visionary guidance of patrons, institutional heads, DSW, and student council leads governing USC UIT.

"Student activities are the heartbeat of campus life. USC UIT provides the platform where classroom knowledge transforms into leadership, innovation, and lifelong impact — cultivating world-class programmers, cultural performers, and articulate leaders recognized across national platforms."

— USC UIT Executive Advisory Council, United Group of Institutions

Our Mission

Bridge academic theory with real-world execution through structured support, grants, and mentorship.

Our Vision

A self-governed student ecosystem where innovation flourishes and leaders emerge on national stages.

Our Values

Global excellence, inclusive leadership, experiential learning, and community impact.
