INSTITUTIONAL GOVERNANCE FRAMEWORK

What is USC UIT?

USC UIT (United Student Club — UIT) is the apex governing council for all student activities, innovation chapters, and leadership programs at United Institute of Technology. Every technical guild, hackathon, cultural fest, and campus initiative operates under its central architecture.

TECHNICAL & CULTURAL ARCHITECTURE

All official student chapters at UIT are structured under either the Technical Wing (coding, AI, bootcamps & hackathons) or the Cultural Wing (performing arts, music, theatre & fests).
