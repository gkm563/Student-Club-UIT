Who We Are

What is USC UIT?

USC UIT (United Student Club — UIT) is the central governing body for all student activities at United Institute of Technology. Every club, chapter, hackathon, cultural fest, and leadership program on campus operates under USC UIT.

  • One umbrella, one identity. USC UIT is the single official council that represents all student life at UIT Prayagraj.
  • Two parent wings. All chapters fall under either Developers Club UIT (technical) or Cultural Club UIT (cultural & literary).
  • Executive-led governance. Guided by the United Group advisory board, UIT institutional heads, and student council leadership.
